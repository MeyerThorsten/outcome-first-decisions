# Provenance and IP Hygiene

`Outcome-First Decisions` is an independently authored business-decision skill by Thorsten Meyer.

The package should be maintained as its own work, not as a persona skill, derivative work, summary, or adaptation of any third-party founder, creator, company, book, course, or paid skill package.

## Maintenance Rules

- Do not copy text from third-party skill packages, books, courses, newsletters, interviews, talks, websites, or documentation unless the copied material is clearly licensed for reuse and attribution is included where required.
- Do not import third-party source lists, quote collections, signature phrases, named challenges, or branded frameworks.
- Keep framework names, examples, templates, and operating rules native to this package.
- Use general business concepts only in original wording and arrangement.
- When adding references, prefer the user's own operating principles, original examples, and independently written templates.
- If external inspiration is useful, record it as background research outside the distributed package and rewrite the resulting guidance from first principles.

## Current Package Notes

- The distributed skill is named `outcome-first-decisions`.
- Its operating spine is buyer evidence, one scoreboard number, small proof tests, written keep/change/kill thresholds, and capacity reallocation.
- The package contains no intended third-party persona simulation, endorsement claim, source list, quote collection, or copied branded framework.

## Review Checklist

Before release, search the package for:

- third-party names or brands used as authority
- externally branded framework names
- copied or lightly paraphrased quotes
- source-list files copied from another package
- examples that reproduce another author's distinctive scenario, sequence, or wording

If any are found, remove them or replace them with original Outcome-First wording.
