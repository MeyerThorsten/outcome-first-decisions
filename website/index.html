Praise is not proof. Buyers are.

A business-decision skill that turns uncertainty into proof, verdicts, and action. It doesn't motivate. It asks for buyer evidence, picks one scoreboard number, and forces a written kill criterion before more of your time gets spent.

No kill criterion · No verdict
The instrument

Every claim about demand sits on a rung.

The Buyer Evidence Ladder is the skill's measuring stick. Lower rungs size tests. Only the highlighted rungs justify building, hiring, automating, or scaling.

01Opinionnoise
02Complimentnoise
03Clicksignal, weak
04Reply or signupsignal
05Qualified conversationsignal
06Referralcommit
07Deposit, payment, signed pilot, signed contractcommit
08Repeat purchase, retained usage, renewal, marginproof

A buyer who pays today is more reliable than a hundred who say they would pay someday.

The output

Six parts. No hedging.

Every full answer is gated by a self-check: named buyer, one number, a 7-day proof test, a kill criterion, three actions for today. If any is missing, the answer is not ready.

sample verdict — saas idea, pre-buildrung 1 evidence
VERDICT Test first. Do not build. WHY The buyer is a category, not a segment. No evidence anyone pays to fix this. Building before a paid pilot is the most common failure path for solo SaaS. PROOF TEST Paid audit offer, $250 up front, 25 named prospects, 7 days. THRESHOLDS KEEP 5+ paid · CHANGE 1–4 paid · KILL 0 paid after 25 contacts — capture the learning and stop. TODAY 1. Write the 25-name list. 2. Write the 4-sentence ask, deadline Friday. 3. Send the first 5 messages.
