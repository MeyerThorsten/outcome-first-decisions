# Example: Prioritization

A worked transcript applying the Worth Filter to three competing opportunities. Two get killed.

---

## Prompt

> "I'm a solo consultant doing brand strategy. I'm trying to decide between three things: (1) launch a $2k group coaching program, (2) write a book on positioning, (3) build a paid newsletter. I have maybe 10 hours a week of side capacity. What should I do?"

## Response

**Verdict.** Run the group coaching program. Defer the newsletter. Kill the book.

**Why.** The group program is the only one with a near-term path to revenue, a buyer the user already reaches, and a 7-day proof test. The book is a 12-month bet against an audience the user does not yet have. The newsletter is reasonable but only after a paying audience exists to write to.

**Evidence read.** Worth Filter scores below.

| Option              | Money | Urgency | Reach | Repeatability | Speed | Fit | **Total** |
| ------------------- | ----- | ------- | ----- | ------------- | ----- | --- | --------- |
| Group coaching $2k  | 5     | 4       | 3     | 4             | 5     | 5   | **26**    |
| Book on positioning | 2     | 1       | 2     | 3             | 1     | 4   | **13**    |
| Paid newsletter     | 3     | 2       | 2     | 5             | 3     | 4   | **19**    |

- **Group coaching (26).** Existing client list is a real channel. $2k × 8 seats = $16k in 8 weeks. Cohort can be tested with a 7-day pre-sale. Repeatable.
- **Book (13).** Money slow, urgency low, reach unclear without a platform. 12-18 month build, hardest possible distribution. Defer until a 5,000+ buyer audience exists.
- **Newsletter (19).** Promising but too early. Builds an asset, but with 10 hrs/week, paying buyers come faster from coaching. Revisit at 200+ paying coaching alumni.

**Proof test.** Cash Proof Sprint for the group program, 7 days.

```
Offer:    "Positioning Sharpener" — 6-week group cohort, 8 seats, $2,000.
Buyer:    Past clients + warm referrals (already reachable).
Ask:      $500 deposit by Friday, full $2,000 at kickoff in 14 days.
Channel:  Direct email to existing client list (~80 names).
Window:   7 days.
```

**Keep / change / kill thresholds.**

- **Keep (run cohort):** 5+ deposits ($2,500+ collected) by Friday.
- **Change (refine):** 2-4 deposits + clear pattern in objections. Reshape positioning, retest.
- **Kill:** 0-1 deposits. The list does not want this product. Move 10 hrs/week elsewhere — but not to the book.

**Next three actions (today).**

1. Draft the cohort email. 6 sentences. Lead with the buyer outcome ("you'll have a one-page positioning your buyers can repeat"), end with the deposit ask and Friday deadline.
2. Pull the existing client list. Tag the 25 most likely buyers. Send to those 25 first.
3. Block calendar: 4 hours over the next 48 hours for replies. Reply within 12 hours to every message.

The book stays on a "maybe in 18 months" list, not the active list. The newsletter stays on a "after 200 alumni" list. Neither gets capacity until the cohort is running.

## Why this answer is in this shape

- The Worth Filter forces the comparison numerical. Gut preference for the book gets exposed by Speed = 1 and Money = 2.
- The proof test is a pre-sale, not a survey. Deposits are rung 7 evidence; survey signups are rung 4.
- The deferred options have explicit re-trigger conditions ("after 200 alumni"). Without those, deferred work re-creeps onto the active list within weeks.
- The user asked between three options. The answer says yes to one, no to two. Saying yes to all three with 10 hrs/week is a maybe wearing a strategy costume.
