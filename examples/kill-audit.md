# Example: Kill List Audit

A worked transcript applying the Kill List Audit to a scattered project list. Six items in, three out, two changed.

---

## Prompt

> "I have too much going on. Here's everything: (1) revamp my agency website, (2) finish the YouTube channel intro video, (3) write the Q3 sales playbook, (4) onboard the new VA, (5) pitch the keynote at a conference next month, (6) finalize my partnership with [Vendor X]. What should I cut?"

## Response

**Verdict.** Keep 1 (sales playbook), keep 2 (VA onboarding). Change 1 (partnership). Kill 3 (website, YouTube intro, conference keynote).

**Why.** Three of the six items have no named buyer or scoreboard contribution; two of those three are vanity projects dressed as business work. The sales playbook and VA onboarding directly move the one number (qualified pipeline). The partnership is a real opportunity but its current form has no proof test.

## Audit Table

| # | Item                   | Named buyer            | Owner   | Evidence rung | Path to revenue/learning           | Action  |
| - | ---------------------- | ---------------------- | ------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------- |
| 1 | Revamp agency website  | None ("looks dated")   | User    | Rung 1        | Hypothetical                       | KILL    |
| 2 | YouTube intro video    | None                   | User    | Rung 1        | None — channel has no audience yet | KILL    |
| 3 | Q3 sales playbook      | New AEs                | User    | Rung 6        | Direct lift to close rate          | KEEP    |
| 4 | VA onboarding          | User (capacity return) | User    | Rung 5        | 8 hrs/week reclaimed → pipeline    | KEEP    |
| 5 | Conference keynote     | None ("good exposure") | User    | Rung 2        | Vague distribution bet             | KILL    |
| 6 | Partnership w/ Vendor X| Vendor X's CSMs        | Unclear | Rung 3        | Real but undefined                 | CHANGE  |

## Per-Item Reasoning

**Website revamp — KILL.** No named buyer is asking for it. "Looks dated" is the user's anxiety, not a buyer signal. Last three deals closed without anyone mentioning the site. Capacity returned: ~25 hrs over the next month. Save the brief; revisit when a buyer cites the site as a blocker.

**YouTube intro — KILL.** Channel has 47 subscribers and no offer attached. Producing content for an audience that does not yet exist, on a platform with the highest production cost per view. Capture the script outline; revisit if the channel ever passes 1,000 subscribers with a proof of buyer overlap.

**Sales playbook — KEEP.** New AE starts in 3 weeks. Without the playbook, ramp time stretches by ~60 days. Direct line to qualified pipeline (the scoreboard number). Owner: user, deadline 14 days, proof: AE quota attainment in month 2.

**VA onboarding — KEEP.** Returns 8 hrs/week of user capacity within 30 days. Reclaimed capacity goes to the partnership work and sales playbook, not new commitments. Owner: user, deadline 14 days, proof: 8 hrs/week returned by week 4.

**Partnership — CHANGE.** "Finalize the partnership" is not a goal; it is a meeting. Reframe: "Get 5 named pilot customers introduced through Vendor X's CSMs in 30 days, with a $500/seat referral fee." Real ask, real deadline, real proof. Without the reshape, this stays in maybe-land for another quarter.

**Conference keynote — KILL.** "Good exposure" is rung 2 evidence at best. The audience is wrong (engineers, not buyers); travel + prep is ~30 hrs. Decline the keynote, refer a peer who fits the audience, send a recorded version if asked. Capacity returned: ~30 hrs over the next month.

## Capacity Reclaimed

```text
Killed items:    Website (25 hrs) + YouTube intro (10 hrs) + Conference (30 hrs) = ~65 hrs over next 4 weeks
Going to:        Sales playbook (15 hrs) + VA onboarding (10 hrs) + Partnership reshape (15 hrs) = 40 hrs
Buffer kept:     ~25 hrs unallocated — protected for replies, surprises, or rest.
```

Do not re-spend the buffer on a new commitment. The buffer is the point.

## Captured Learnings (before killing)

- **Website:** "I keep wanting to redo the site because I'm avoiding harder buyer-facing work." Pattern to remember: polish creep precedes most kill audits.
- **YouTube:** "I confused 'wanting a content channel' with 'having an audience.' Build the audience before the production system."
- **Conference:** "Exposure is rung 2. I will require rung 6+ named-pipeline evidence before saying yes to speaking gigs in the next 12 months."

## Next Three Actions (today)

1. Email Vendor X: reframe the partnership ask to 5 named pilot intros in 30 days at $500/seat. Deadline for their reply: 5 business days.
2. Decline the conference keynote. Refer a peer if a fit. Send the email today.
3. Block 90 minutes tomorrow morning to write the sales playbook outline. The website tab stays closed.
