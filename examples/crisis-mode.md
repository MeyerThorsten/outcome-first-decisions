# Example: Crisis Mode

A worked transcript of the collapsed output shape. Runway under 90 days triggers Crisis Mode automatically — no Worth Filter tables, no Evidence Ladder discussion, no multi-paragraph reasoning. Verdict, three actions with hour-level deadlines, the dollar kill threshold. Nothing else.

---

## Prompt

> "We have about 70 days of cash left. $11k in the bank, burn is roughly $4.8k/month. I'm mid-way through rebuilding our onboarding flow, I have a conference talk in three weeks, and I've been working on a new pricing page. Two of our eight customers churned last month. What do I do?"

## Response

**Verdict.** Stop all three projects today. The only work that exists for the next 14 days is collecting cash from people who have already paid you.

**Three actions for today.**

1. **By 12:00 — list the six remaining customers** with contract value, renewal date, and the last date each got a result they could screenshot. The two most retention-risky get called first.
2. **By 15:00 — call (not email) the three strongest customers** with a prepay offer: 12 months at 2 months free, invoice today, payment this week. Script: "We're concentrating on our best customers this quarter. I want you locked in at the current rate."
3. **By 17:00 — message the two churned customers** with one question: "What would have had to be true for you to stay?" Their answers decide what the six survivors hear next week.

**Kill threshold.** Less than **$9,600 collected in 14 days** (two annual prepays) → begin wind-down or radical-pivot decision on day 15, while there is still cash to do it deliberately. Write the threshold down now, before the calls — it does not get renegotiated after a friendly conversation.

---

## What got skipped, on purpose

The onboarding rebuild, the talk, and the pricing page were not scored, compared, or sympathized with. In crisis, anything that cannot produce cash inside 14 days is paused in one move — the full output shape is itself busywork at 70 days of runway. The conference talk may be a fine idea in the quarter that starts after the threshold is met.
