# Offer One-Pager — Template

A sharp offer in five fields, plus a pre-sale ask. Reads in 30 seconds. Sells in one breath.

Fill in for a single offer. If the same template doesn't work for two offers, the offers are different and need separate one-pagers.

## The Five Fields

```text
BUYER:
[Specific person or segment. Not a category. Include role, scale, or stage signal.]

MOMENT:
[The trigger that makes the problem urgent now. A change in their world, not yours.]

PROMISE:
[The measurable or visible outcome. Number, date, or observable change.
 Banned: "feel better," "have clarity," "be confident."]

MECHANISM:
[The credible why-we. The specific approach that produces the result. One sentence.]

ASK:
[A pre-sale commitment ask. Use a pattern from outreach/pre-sale-ask-language.md.
 Banned: "book a call," "let me know if you're interested."]
```

## Rejection Conditions

Don't ship a one-pager that fails any of these:

- **Buyer** is broader than ~10,000 reachable people in a specific role/segment → narrow until reachable.
- **Moment** is the user's worry, not the buyer's recent change → rewrite from the buyer's calendar.
- **Promise** is unmeasurable, vague, or aspirational → quantify or strike.
- **Mechanism** is the same as the promise repeated → name the *how*, not the *what*.
- **Ask** is generic ("interested?", "book a call") → swap to a pre-sale pattern with price, deadline, refund policy.

## Filled Example — Services

```text
BUYER:
Series-A B2B SaaS founders scaling onboarding from 12 to 40 customers/month, currently with 1-2 customer-success hires.

MOMENT:
Q3 ramp targets just locked; existing onboarding adds 14 days to time-to-value and is showing up as churn risk in 9-month renewal data.

PROMISE:
Cut onboarding time-to-value from 21 days to 7 days within 60 days, measured in your existing analytics.

MECHANISM:
14-day audit of your last 10 onboardings, identifying the 3 hand-off points where time leaks; one-page tightening plan with named owners and Q3 implementation deadlines.

ASK:
$2,500 for the 60-day pilot, paid before kickoff. If we don't hit time-to-value < 14 days by day 60, you keep the audit + plan and 50% of the fee refunds. Two slots open for May. Reply by Friday.
```

## Filled Example — Creator / Cohort

```text
BUYER:
Solo agency owners doing $20k-$80k MRR, currently re-positioning after a category shift in their market.

MOMENT:
Recent revenue plateau or churn cluster from buyers who can't articulate "what you do" to their team or their boss.

PROMISE:
A one-page positioning your buyers can repeat without you in the room — measured against 10 actual buyer quotes by week 6.

MECHANISM:
6-week cohort: weekly working session with 8 peers, structured Buyer-Problem-Path interrogation, paid pilot homework with 3 of your real prospects.

ASK:
$2,000, 8 seats, starts May 14. $500 deposit by Friday holds the seat. If we don't reach 5 paid seats by Friday, the cohort moves to June and your deposit refunds in 3 days.
```

## Filled Example — SaaS Pre-Sale

```text
BUYER:
Heads of Revenue Ops at $20M-$100M ARR B2B companies running Salesforce + 3+ revenue point-tools.

MOMENT:
End-of-quarter forecast accuracy below 75%; CRO under pressure; tooling consolidation is on the FY agenda.

PROMISE:
Forecast accuracy ≥ 85% within two quarters, with one fewer point-tool to maintain.

MECHANISM:
A unified pipeline-hygiene engine plugged into Salesforce — designed in the open with three founding customers, delivered by [date], priced at half list for 12 months.

ASK:
12-month annual contract at $24k (50% off list), paid up front. Three founding-customer slots open. LOI by [date]; we ship the v1 by [date 60 days out] or your prepayment becomes credit on any other capability for 12 months.
```

## After Filling

Test the one-pager by sending it to 10-25 buyers via `outreach/cold-outreach.md`. Track replies by Evidence Ladder rung. Stop counting at rung 6+; below that is signal-to-sharpen-the-next-version.

If 25 contacts produce 0 rung-6+ commitments, the one-pager is not the problem — the buyer or moment is. Don't iterate on the language; iterate on who you're sending it to.

Log the verdict to the decision journal using the YAML schema in `decision-journal/decision-log-format.md`, with `category: offer`.
