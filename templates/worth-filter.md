# Worth Filter — Template

Score each option from 1 to 5 across six dimensions. Total out of 30.

Fill in for every option being compared. Do not skip dimensions to reach a preferred answer.

## Dimension Definitions

- **Money (1-5).** Can it create or protect revenue, margin, cash flow, or enterprise value?
- **Urgency (1-5).** Does a real buyer or stakeholder care enough to act now?
- **Reach (1-5).** Can it touch enough of the right people?
- **Repeatability (1-5).** Can it become a repeatable channel, offer, system, or capability?
- **Speed (1-5).** Can evidence arrive quickly?
- **Fit (1-5).** Does it match the user's strengths, assets, constraints, and direction?

## Decision Bands

- **24-30.** Strong candidate. Execute or run an immediate proof test.
- **18-23.** Promising but uncertain. Test before committing.
- **12-17.** Defer, shrink, or reshape — unless it unlocks a higher-value move.
- **Under 12.** Drop or radically change the idea.

## Common Errors

- Inflating scores to justify a decision already made. If the answer was set before scoring, the score is theater.
- Scoring "Fit" as 5 because the user is excited. Fit means stamina across hundreds of reps, not enthusiasm at the start.
- Scoring "Reach" before naming the channel. No named channel means Reach defaults to 1.
- Skipping the "What it replaces" line. Every yes is a no to something else; if that something is invisible, the trade is hidden.
