# Industry Overlay: SaaS

Load when the user's offer is software with recurring revenue.

Apply on top of the parent skill's defaults. Replace the generic Worth Filter and Cash Proof Sprint inputs with the signals below where applicable.

## Unit of Value

**Net new ARR per cohort, weighted by gross retention at month 6.**

Not MRR alone, not signups, not pipeline. New revenue that survives the first cohort window. A SaaS that books $100k of ARR and loses 60% in 6 months is not a $100k business; it is a $40k business with a leaky bucket and a marketing addiction.

## Most-Believed-But-Wrong Narrative

**"We need more leads."**

Almost always false at the seed and early stages. The real problem is usually one of:

- **Activation.** Signups never reach the moment of value.
- **Specificity.** The buyer cannot tell whether the product is for them.
- **Pricing.** The price-to-value frame discourages serious buyers.
- **Retention.** New revenue is replacing churned revenue, not growing.

Run the cohort retention chart before approving any "more leads" project.

## Buyer-Behavior Signals (rung 6+)

1. **Self-serve activation within 14 days.** A new signup completes the workflow that produces value, without a sales call. Aggregate as "% of week-1 signups who reach core action by day 14."
2. **Logo expansion.** A paying account adds seats, modules, or contract value within the first 6 months — without a discount.
3. **Inbound referral.** A customer introduces a named buyer at another company by name and email, not "you should check out X."

## Hardest-to-Fake Proof Test

**Annual prepay at full list price for a feature that does not yet exist.**

Offer a buyer a 12-month prepaid contract at full list price for a defined capability with a 60-day delivery deadline. If three buyers prepay, the demand is real and the build is justified. If none do, "interest" was rung 2.

## Common SaaS Anti-Patterns to Watch

- **Free tier without an activation gate.** Free users who never reach value distort every metric.
- **Roadmap selling.** Promising features in 6 months instead of selling what works today.
- **Vanity ARR.** Booking annual contracts paid monthly without termination clauses; counting at-risk revenue as won.
- **Founder-led sales hand-waving.** "I closed 5 deals last quarter" — none of which closed without the founder. Not a repeatable pattern, not a sales motion.

## Scoreboard Defaults for SaaS Users

If the user cannot pick one number, default to one of:

- Weekly net new ARR (at full list, no annual prepay discounts above 10%).
- Cohort retention at month 6 (% of month-1 ARR still paying).
- Activation rate (% of new signups reaching core action by day 14).

Pick whichever is currently the bottleneck per Bottleneck Before Breadth.
