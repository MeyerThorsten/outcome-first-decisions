# Industry Overlay: Healthcare

Load when the user's business delivers or supports care — private practices, clinics, therapy, digital health, med-adjacent services, or tools sold into provider organizations.

Apply on top of the parent skill's defaults. Replace the generic Worth Filter and Cash Proof Sprint inputs with the signals below where applicable. Note: clinical claims, privacy, and advertising in healthcare are regulated; proof tests below are commercial tests and must stay inside the user's regulatory boundaries — flag this explicitly in any verdict.

## Unit of Value

**Reimbursed or collected revenue per completed care episode, weighted by patient retention or referral.**

Not appointment volume, not app downloads, not pilot conversations. In healthcare the gap between "delivered" and "paid" is where businesses die: a clinic full of un-reimbursed visits or a digital-health tool in unpaid pilots is running a charity with venture overhead.

## Most-Believed-But-Wrong Narrative

**"Providers love it — we just need to get through procurement."**

Usually false as a diagnosis. Provider enthusiasm is rung 1–2; healthcare buys on budget authority, billing pathway, and risk. The real problem is usually one of:

- **No payer path.** Nobody has named the CPT code, budget line, or out-of-pocket willingness that pays for it.
- **Champion without authority.** The enthusiastic clinician cannot sign, and the signer has different incentives (cost, liability, throughput).
- **Workflow tax.** The solution adds minutes to a clinician's day; adoption dies quietly regardless of outcomes.
- **Pilot purgatory.** Unpaid or discounted pilots that renew as pilots because nothing forces a buy decision.

Name who pays, from which budget, via which mechanism, before any build or hire.

## Buyer-Behavior Signals (rung 6+)

1. **Paid through the real mechanism.** A claim reimbursed, an invoice paid from a named budget, or a patient paying out of pocket at list price — not a discounted pilot fund.
2. **Renewal or episode completion with rebooking.** A patient completes the care plan and rebooks, or an organization renews after the pilot at equal or higher price.
3. **Clinical champion spends political capital.** A clinician presents the solution to their own leadership or peers unprompted, by name, in writing.

## Hardest-to-Fake Proof Test

**Paid pilot with a pre-signed conversion clause.**

For B2B: a 60–90 day paid pilot (real money, real budget line) whose contract names the conversion price and the metric threshold that triggers it — signed before the pilot starts. For practices/direct care: sell a defined care package at full price to the next 20 eligible patients. If the pilot converts or 5+ packages sell, the payer path is real. If everyone wants it free, there is no buyer yet.

## Common Healthcare Anti-Patterns to Watch

- **Free-pilot collecting.** A logo slide of unpaid pilots presented as traction.
- **Selling outcomes to the wrong incentive.** Pitching better care to a buyer paid on throughput or cost.
- **Compliance as a moat claim.** Treating certification work as progress while no one has committed to pay.
- **Build-for-the-demo.** Features that win clinician applause but add workflow minutes.
- **Ignoring the no-show economics.** For practices: growth spend while unfilled and no-show slots burn the margin.

## Scoreboard Defaults for Healthcare Users

If the user cannot pick one number, default to one of:

- Weekly collected revenue (cash or cleared claims, not billed).
- Paid-pilot conversion rate (% of pilots converting at the pre-signed price).
- Completed-episode rebooking rate (% of patients finishing a care plan who rebook or refer).

Pick whichever is currently the bottleneck per Bottleneck Before Breadth.
