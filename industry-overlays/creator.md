# Industry Overlay: Creator

Load when the user monetizes audience attention — newsletter, YouTube, podcast, course, paid community, or content-led product.

## Unit of Value

**Recurring buyer per 1,000 attention units, weighted by 12-month retention.**

Audience size alone is vanity. A 50k newsletter with 200 paying subscribers at $20/month is a $48k/year business. A 5k newsletter with 800 paying subscribers at $20/month is a $192k/year business. Compare buyer density, not subscriber count.

## Most-Believed-But-Wrong Narrative

**"I need a bigger audience."**

Usually false until conversion, retention, and offer are sharpened. The real bottleneck is usually one of:

- **No buyer offer.** The audience consumes free content but has nothing to buy.
- **Wrong buyer.** The audience overlaps poorly with the offer's buyer segment.
- **Conversion mechanics.** The path from free to paid is unclear, slow, or buried.
- **Retention.** New paying buyers leave before contributing meaningful LTV.

Audit buyer density and 12-month retention before approving any "grow the list" project.

## Buyer-Behavior Signals (rung 6+)

1. **Paid renewal at month 12.** A subscriber re-pays a year-long product without a discount or "save" offer.
2. **Buyer-to-buyer referral.** A paying subscriber tells a named friend by name, who then pays — visible in referral attribution.
3. **Cross-product purchase.** A buyer of one product (course, community, paid newsletter) buys a second product within 6 months.

## Hardest-to-Fake Proof Test

**Pre-sell the next paid product to existing audience before it exists.**

Announce a paid product (cohort, course, premium tier) with full price, a 14-day pre-sale window, and a 60-day delivery date. If 5% of the engaged list pre-pays, the offer-buyer fit is real. If under 1% pre-pays, the audience does not match this product — even if it is large.

The pre-sale must be at full price. Discounts at this stage hide the answer.

## Common Creator Anti-Patterns to Watch

- **Production over distribution.** Investing in higher production value before reaching more of the right buyer.
- **Multiple paid products.** Three half-built products instead of one fully-built one.
- **Audience-first thinking.** Building reach before knowing what the audience will buy.
- **Sponsorship dependency.** Optimizing for ad/sponsor revenue when buyer-direct revenue would compound.
- **Algorithm chasing.** Reshaping the offer for whichever platform is currently rewarding.

## Scoreboard Defaults for Creator Users

Default to one of:

- Paying buyers (active, last 30 days).
- Buyer density (paying buyers / 1,000 audience units).
- 12-month buyer retention rate.

Pick whichever is currently the bottleneck.
