# Industry Overlay: Services / Agency

Load when the user delivers labor-leveraged work — consulting, agency, freelance, or productized services.

## Unit of Value

**Margin per hour of expert time, weighted by retention beyond the first engagement.**

Revenue alone is misleading in services. A $50k engagement that consumes 200 expert hours at $250 looks the same on the books as one delivered in 60 hours through a tight method. They are not the same business.

## Most-Believed-But-Wrong Narrative

**"We need to hire more people."**

Usually false until pricing, scope, and method are sharpened. Hiring before margin is repeatable creates a more expensive version of the same problem. The real bottleneck is usually one of:

- **Scope creep.** Engagements expand without price changes.
- **Process drift.** Each engagement reinvents the method.
- **Buyer mismatch.** The agency is selling to buyers who require heavy education or revisions.
- **Founder bottleneck.** Senior judgment is on every deliverable, regardless of value.

Audit margin per engagement before approving any hire.

## Buyer-Behavior Signals (rung 6+)

1. **Multi-engagement client.** A buyer commits to a second engagement at the same or higher price within 90 days of the first delivery.
2. **Referral with named buyer + email.** Not "you should reach out to," but "I told [Name] you'd email them on [day]."
3. **Acceptance of fixed-fee scope.** A buyer signs a fixed-fee SOW (not hourly), with a defined deliverable and timeline. This signals confidence in method.

## Hardest-to-Fake Proof Test

**Productized engagement at a fixed price, sold without a discovery call.**

Define a 4-6 week engagement with a clear deliverable, a fixed price, and a named outcome. Sell three of them off the existing client list or warm network without scoping calls. If three buyers buy, the productization is real and the agency can scale on it. If none do, the offer needs sharpening, not more leads.

## Common Services Anti-Patterns to Watch

- **Hourly billing on packaged work.** Anchors the buyer to inputs instead of outcomes.
- **"We do everything" positioning.** Forces the buyer to imagine the value.
- **Founder on every project.** Margin caps at the founder's hours.
- **Discounting to close.** A discount given before any pushback signals weak pricing confidence and trains the buyer to expect more.
- **Free strategy work.** Prospect "discovery" that delivers real value before the deal closes.

## Scoreboard Defaults for Services Users

Default to one of:

- Margin per engagement (revenue minus delivery cost, divided by senior hours).
- Repeat engagement rate (% of clients booking a second engagement within 90 days).
- Time-to-cash from inbound (days from first contact to deposit cleared).

Pick whichever is currently the bottleneck.
