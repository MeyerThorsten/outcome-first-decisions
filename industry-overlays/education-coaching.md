# Industry Overlay: Education / Coaching

Load when the user sells learning or transformation — courses, cohorts, coaching, training, certification programs, or workshops.

Apply on top of the parent skill's defaults. Replace the generic Worth Filter and Cash Proof Sprint inputs with the signals below where applicable.

## Unit of Value

**Completed outcomes per cohort, weighted by full-price re-enrollment or referral within 90 days.**

Not enrollments, not course sales, not list size. An education business is valued by whether buyers finish and get the promised result — completion and outcome drive referrals, renewals, and pricing power. A program with 500 buyers and 4% completion is a refund liability with good marketing.

## Most-Believed-But-Wrong Narrative

**"I need a bigger audience before I can launch."**

Almost always false. Education sells on specificity and proof, not reach. The real problem is usually one of:

- **Outcome vagueness.** The program promises understanding instead of a verifiable result by a date.
- **Wrong buyer moment.** Selling to the mildly curious instead of people with a deadline, stakes, or trigger event.
- **No delivery proof.** Zero documented befores-and-afters, so price stays low and ads stay expensive.
- **Format-first thinking.** Choosing "course" before checking whether the buyer pays more for done-with-you.

Ten buyers at full price from a small list beat a thousand followers every time.

## Buyer-Behavior Signals (rung 6+)

1. **Full-price enrollment with a deadline attached.** A buyer pays the listed price for a dated cohort or program — not a lifetime-access discount bundle.
2. **Completion plus documented result.** A participant finishes and reports a concrete outcome (number, artifact, credential, role change) attributable to the program.
3. **Alumni re-purchase or named referral.** A graduate buys the next tier or sends a named, paying enrollee within 90 days.

## Hardest-to-Fake Proof Test

**Pre-sell a dated cohort at full price before any curriculum exists.**

Define the outcome, the dates, the price, and a cap (e.g., 8 seats). Offer it to the existing list or warm network with payment up front and a refund policy. If 5+ buyers pay before week's end, build the curriculum live with cohort one. If 0–1 pay, the promised outcome or buyer moment is wrong — no amount of content production fixes that.

## Common Education Anti-Patterns to Watch

- **Content treadmill.** Producing free material indefinitely as a substitute for making an ask.
- **Pre-launch curriculum building.** Months of recording and platform setup before a single paid enrollment.
- **Lifetime-access discounting.** Killing urgency and cohort dynamics to chase one-time volume.
- **Vanity certificates.** Credentials no employer or client asks for, used to pad the promise.
- **Audience-building as procrastination.** Growing followers while declining to sell anything to them.

## Scoreboard Defaults for Education Users

If the user cannot pick one number, default to one of:

- Cash collected per cohort launch (full-price enrollments × price, refunds netted).
- Completion-to-outcome rate (% of enrollees finishing with a documented result).
- Alumni revenue share (% of revenue from re-enrollment and named referrals).

Pick whichever is currently the bottleneck per Bottleneck Before Breadth.
